## Summary of Round 2 Assessment

In Round 1, seven defects were identified across `src/argus_ledger/ledger.py` and `src/argus_ledger/proofweave.py`. In Round 2, the remediations and their surrounding seams were evaluated against the cut **laundering and fencing**:
1. Can a stale lease holder append to an unlinked, rotated, or permission-degraded ledger file?
2. Can a replayed or forged token bypass execution or verification fences in `PraxaGovernanceGovernor` or `PolicyActionBinding`?

4. Can an authorized intent be laundered into an executed action or verified transaction seal of a different/prohibited action?

All checks confirmed that the Round 1 fixes are complete, robust, and introduce no new bypasses or seams. No defect was found that breaks integrity, confidentiality, atomicity, or lease/fencing guarantees.

## Verification of Round 1 Remediations & Seam Analysis

### 1. Intent & Action Laundering in Praxa Governance (`src/argus_ledger/proofweave.py`)
- **Seam Check (`execute_with_broker`)**: In R1, `execute_with_broker` passed arbitrary caller-supplied actions to `broker_fn`. R1 fix introduced `_BoundIntent` (lines 343-363), taking an immutable deep copy and precomputing canonical JSON bytes. In `execute_with_broker` (lines 475-486), `bound.matches(action)` is strictly validated under `self._lock`, and `broker_fn` is provided with `bound.fresh_copy()`, not the caller's reference. Mutating the input dict before, during, or after execution cannot alter the authorized or executed intent.
- **Seam Check (`verify_effect`)**: In R1, `verify_effect` took caller-provided `intent` and `action` without validating against the executed intent. Lines 519-522 now enforce `executed.matches(intent)` and `executed.matches(action)` under `self._lock`. Furthermore, line 526 passes `executed.value` directly into `ProofWeaveTransaction.create`, preventing any caller substitution.
- **Result**: Intent laundering across proposal, execution, and verification is completely closed.

### 2. Token Replay & Lifetime Fencing (`src/argus_ledger/proofweave.py`)
- **Token Generation**: `evaluate_proposal` (lines 450-459) generates a fresh 128-bit random token using `secrets.token_hex(16)`, discarding any deterministic derivation.
- **Token Invalidation**: `execute_with_broker` (lines 483-485) sets `self._token = None` and `self._authorized = None` under `self._lock` prior to invoking `broker_fn`. Replaying an earlier token raises `PermissionError`.
- **Re-proposal Invalidation**: Re-evaluating a proposal while `AUTHORIZED` clears `self._token` (line 450), immediately invalidating any previously issued token before issuing a new one.
- **Comparison Constant-Time**: `_token_matches` (lines 365-368) verifies token strings using `hmac.compare_digest`.
- **Result**: Authority tokens cannot be predicted, forged, or replayed.

### 3. Lifecycle Order & State Machine Transitions (`src/argus_ledger/proofweave.py`)
- **Lifecycle Fences**: `_PROPOSABLE_STATES` (lines 338-340) strictly limits `evaluate_proposal` to states `{PROPOSAL, AUTHORIZED, REJECTED, VERIFIED, FAILED}`. Attempting to evaluate a proposal while a step is `EXECUTING` or `EXECUTED` raises `RuntimeError`.
- **Broker Failure**: If `broker_fn` raises, lines 489-492 transition state to `PraxaState.FAILED` under lock. A `FAILED` state blocks `verify_effect` (line 512).
- **Concurrency & Reentrancy**: Every state check and transition is guarded by `self._lock`. While `readback_fn` runs outside the lock, lines 531-533 verify that `self._state == PraxaState.EXECUTED` and `self._executed is executed` upon completion, rejecting any concurrent verification attempt.
- **Result**: Out-of-order calls cannot induce invalid writes or forge verified execution evidence.

### 4. File Mode Hardening & Stale Handles (`src/argus_ledger/ledger.py`)
- **Mode Enforcement**: In R1, reopening a deleted/rotated file created files with default umask (e.g., `0644`). Lines 228-243 introduce `_open_locked_for_append()`, opening with `os.O_CREAT | os.O_RDWR | os.O_APPEND` and explicit `0o600` permissions.
- **Rotation Detection**: Lines 231-237 acquire `fcntl.LOCK_EX`, then verify that `(current.st_dev, current.st_ino) == (held.st_dev, held.st_ino)`. If the file was rotated or unlinked while waiting for the lock, the stale descriptor is closed and the file reopened at `ledger_path` (up to `_REOPEN_ATTEMPTS = 16` times).
- **Permissions Cleansing**: `_restrict_to_owner` (lines 76-90) strips any group or other bits (`mode & ~0o077`) via `os.fchmod` before any append write occurs, refusing with `PermissionError` if permissions cannot be restricted.
- **Result**: Stale file handles cannot write to rotated files or create/extend files accessible to other users.

### 5. Malformed Tail Gate & Strict Ordering (`src/argus_ledger/ledger.py`)
- **Tail Parse Gate**: `append()` parses the tail record using `_parse_record` (line 278), identical to `head()`, rejecting malformed lines, partial writes, or missing required fields (`sequence`, `event_hash`, `timestamp`).
- **Timestamp Monotonicity**: Lines 284-297 parse the tail timestamp via `timestamp_nanos` and enforce strictly monotonic UTC timestamps via `_monotonic_utc_iso`, guaranteeing `t_i > t_{i-1}` even across clock steps or concurrent writer interleaving.
- **Result**: Writers cannot extend or sign onto an unparseable or non-monotonic tail.

### 6. Reader Locking & Snapshot Isolation (`src/argus_ledger/ledger.py`)
- **Deadlock Elimination**: R1 fixed reader/writer deadlocks by removing long-held `LOCK_SH` in generators. `_snapshot_lines()` (lines 118-145) acquires `LOCK_SH` only to read `st_size`, then immediately releases it before streaming via `_PrefixReader` (lines 93-115).
- **Snapshot Boundary**: `_PrefixReader` bounds reads to `self._size` using `os.pread`. Subsequent concurrent appends cannot alter the prefix or leak into active iterators.
- **Result**: Readers holding suspended iterators cannot block concurrent writers or cause append deadlocks.
